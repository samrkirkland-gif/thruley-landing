The same hours in a day.

In force · Queensland1 August 2025

Disclosure became a new starting point.

Sellers generally need to give buyers a completed Form 2 and prescribed certificates before the buyer signs. Exceptions apply, and evidence of delivery matters.

Queensland Government
In force · Australia1 July 2026

AML/CTF added another layer.

Businesses providing designated real estate services now have AML/CTF duties, including customer checks, a risk-based program, reporting and record keeping.

AUSTRAC commencement update
In force · Since 1 August 2025A right to terminate

Missing disclosure can put a sale at risk.

Under Queensland’s disclosure scheme, a buyer may be able to end the contract before settlement if documents are missing. Inaccurate or incomplete disclosure has additional statutory tests.

Read the disclosure requirements
Upcoming · Queensland1 January 2027

Smoke alarms reach the remaining homes.

The rollout extends interconnected photoelectric alarms to all remaining existing private dwellings. Homes being sold or leased have already been subject to requirements since 2022.

Queensland Fire Department
Upcoming · AustraliaJuly–September 2027

The first annual AML report comes next.

AUSTRAC’s next annual compliance report window runs from 1 July to 30 September 2027, covering the 2026–27 financial year. Keep the evidence of how your program operates now.

AUSTRAC reporting guidance
Upcoming · Newly regulated businesses2029–2030

Your first independent AML evaluation.

Transitional deadlines are staggered from 30 June 2029 to 31 December 2030, based on the final two digits of your AUSTRAC account number. Check your date and plan the evaluation.

AUSTRAC transitional rules
